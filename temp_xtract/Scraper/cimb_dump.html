What is eGIA?

A Gold Investment Account is a type of investment account that allows you to buy, hold, and sell gold as part of your investment portfolio.

It is an account that allows you to trade (buy and sell) gold online without having to hold any physical gold.

Why Should You Consider Gold Investment?

Safe Haven Asset

Gold is considered a safe haven due to its history of preserving value during economic uncertainty, market volatility, or geopolitical tensions.

Hedge Against Inflation

When inflation rises, the value of money decreases, but gold tends to maintain or increase in value.

Liquid Asset

Gold is easy to buy and sell in global markets, making it a convenient asset for investors like central banks and hedge funds.

Diversification

Adding gold can help diversify your investment portfolio as gold usually has little correlation with stocks and bonds, reducing overall risk.

Inversely Correlated to the U.S. Dollar

Gold is often inversely correlated to the U.S. dollar, meaning that when the value of the U.S. dollar falls, the price of gold tends to rise, and vice versa.
